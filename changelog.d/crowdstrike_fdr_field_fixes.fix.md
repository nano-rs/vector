The `crowdstrike_fdr` source, after a field trial on a real feed: `file_concurrency` now defaults to 16 (1 left the pipeline idle), with a new `max_memory_bytes` (default 512 MiB) capping memory for objects in total; objects that do not fit spool to `data_dir`. The source's metrics (`crowdstrike_fdr_manifests_total` for every status, files and bytes fetched, the new `crowdstrike_fdr_files_skipped_total`, and the in-flight gauges) are registered at zero at startup and no longer expire. Every batch logs at info when it is received, delivered and deleted, or released (with the reason). The startup log names where batch progress is kept, and warns loudly when the data directory is not persistent (a container's writable layer or a tmpfs), since a restart then re-emits held batches. A new `skip_action` (`delete`, the default, or `release`) lets `start_from` hand older batches back instead of deleting them. The acknowledgements warning now points at the sinks' `acknowledgements.enabled` (NAN-5005).
